About Me

I’m Maryam Alhashmi, a student at NYU Abu Dhabi with an interest in creative media, design, technology, and storytelling. I like working on projects that mix visuals, interaction, and personality, whether that is through a website, a video, a poster, or a digital story.

My skills are a mix of technical and creative tools. I enjoy both the logical side of building something and the creative side of making it feel personal, polished, and expressive.

This portfolio shows the work I created in Communications Lab, but it also shows my style. I wanted it to feel warm, playful, and personal instead of looking like a plain portfolio template.
